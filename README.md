# VA Health Tracker

An offline, single-file tracker for logging migraine (DC 8100), tinnitus (DC 6260) and sciatica (DC 8520) symptoms day by day, to support VA disability claims.

## Use it

Open `index.html` in any browser, or visit the GitHub Pages site if it's enabled for this repo. Click any day in the year grid to log intensity, symptoms, functional impact and notes.

## Your data

Entries are saved only in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent anywhere. Use the JSON or CSV export buttons regularly to keep a backup, and Import to restore it.

## Disclaimer

This log is supporting evidence, not medical or legal advice. Combine it with your medical records and DBQs when filing a claim.
